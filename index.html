StreetGP (STGP): A bike concept many people look down on

Bikes are something I could talk about without getting bored. STGP is for riders who love to speed with their guardian angel and aren't afraid of their bikes getting wrecked, the kind of riders people call "Kamote", and I'm proudly one of them. It is a small community with love for the art of building.

What is STGP?

STGP is a way of customizing small bikes so they look like a big MotoGP bike.

What bikes are valid?

In STGP bikes, the requirement is that the bike itself is an underbone, but it depends on the community or founder.

The “Kamote” problem

"Kamote" is slang for riders with bad habits on the road. This label sticks to the whole community.

My take

It's speed and racing, my guardian angel, and showing the other side of STGP, from the build to the skill of the riders, and showing the world that STGP is not just a "Kamote" community, but a community with values.
